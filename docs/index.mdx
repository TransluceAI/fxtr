---
title: "What is the Transluce platform?"
---

Transluce's platform provides the unified technology stack for developing AI evaluations and analyses. We enable teams of scientists to collaborate with agents at remarkable speed and efficiency, without losing understanding of scientific results.

The platform is built upon a simple philosophy: we provide scientists full control and visibility into core experimental design — including its logic, data, and visualizations — then solve the remaining generic but critical operational problems: reproducibility; trustworthy and verifiable provenance; performant, reliable, and scalable execution; artifact storage and versioning; sharing and collaboration.

There are three layers to the stack:
1. **`fxtr`** is a modular computation and data management framework, purpose-built for AI science
2. **`behaviors`** provides a library of primitives commonly used in AI behavior evaluations
3. The **studio+hub** (coming soon) is a managed service that helps you store, execute, view, and share experiments
